Biography Snapshot

Rick Shaw is a Welsh comedian, mimic and comedic imitator with roots in 1980s and 1990s stage and television performances. His upcoming "Nostalgic Knights" material mixes classic impressions, old-school banter and character-led humour designed to keep audiences laughing.
