Biography

Rick Shaw (stage name) is a hugely talented Stand-Up Comedian, Mimic and Comedic Imitator who first burst onto the comedy scene in the 1980’s / 1990’s winning Welsh Impressionist of the Year, appearing with Russell Grant and Anne Aston (The Golden Shot / Bob Monkhouse) on the television programme ‘All Kinds of Everything’.

Rick Shaw was also one of Fagin’s boys in Lional Bart’s Oliver Twist and in the 1980’s was the manic front man for Wales’ Top Comedy Showband. Their two-hour show was a mainframe choice in entertaining British Officers and their partners at Christmas time, celebrating their Annual Regiments’ Dinner/Dance in Germany.

Rick Shaw Comedy — Welsh impressionist, mimic and comedian. Live variety built around Nostalgic Knights, with booking and enquiries through Contacts.

Note: Some material references older comedy styles and may not suit every taste. Performances are aimed at adult audiences.
