Nostalgic Days

Celebrity and nostalgia-led impressions — comedy, music and entertainment from shared memory.

If you're under the age of 40 you won't even remember many of these celebrity characters. Nevertheless, they all have their part to play in my life and my treasured memories of the past 50 years. Superstars in their own right they remain amongst the world's most treasured celebrities from comedy, film, music, sport and politics.

Sit-Down Comedy

Will these days ever be repeated. I grew up loving and admiring these people — they are part of my life. I still have my 'Walkman' from yesteryear and the majority of these music greats are included on the tracks. If you just love celebrating the 60's, 70's and 80's these 'mimic' songs will bring back loads of treasured memories for you from those eras.
