About

Rick Shaw is the main comedian and performer behind the character universe in the brief. He plays the caricatures, alter-egos and nostalgic impersonations across these acts.

This concept leans into Barnum-style theatrical poster design: dramatic contrast, rich reds and royal blues, gold highlights and bold headline typography.
