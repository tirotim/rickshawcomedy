Fame Edin-Bra Beverage

As: Dame Edna Everage

Rick Shaw's alter ego Fame Edin-Bra Beverage is based on Barry Humphries' iconic Australian housewife and megastar Dame Edna Everage.

Known for lilac wig styling, flamboyant spectacles and the greeting 'Hello Possums', this character celebrates a cult comedy legend loved by UK audiences.

Rick Shaw performs this as one of his signature nostalgic characters in the Nostalgic Knights set.
