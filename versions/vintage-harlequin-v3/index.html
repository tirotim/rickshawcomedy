About Rick Shaw

Rick Shaw is a mimic, imitator and comedian with roots in stage and TV from the 1980s and 1990s, now returning with a retro-first comedy experience for over-40 audiences.

The Nostalgic Knights set celebrates classic British comedy voices, alter-egos and memory-lane entertainment.
