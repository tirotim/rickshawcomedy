Breast Cancer Care Now (Cymru)

Breast cancer care in Wales is provided through NHS Wales, offering screening for women aged 50–70 via Breast Test Wales every three years. Specialist care is provided by local health boards, such as the Cardiff and Vale Breast Centre, while organizations like Breast Cancer Now and Tenovus provide support.

Key services and support in Wales

  • Screening: Women 50–70 are invited automatically. Women over 70 can request screening by contacting Breast Test Wales.
  • Support and information: Breast Cancer Now (0808 800 6000) and Tenovus Cancer Care (0808 808 1010) offer helplines, support groups and information.
  • Breast centres: Dedicated units such as the Cardiff and Vale Breast Centre offer diagnostic, treatment and support services.
  • Welsh language: Support services frequently offer bilingual care, including Tenovus and local health board services.

Maggie’s

Find help and information from professional staff, or join groups and classes to make coping with cancer easier. We’re here with you every step of the way.

You don’t need to live near a Maggie’s centre to get cancer support. Search for your nearest centre geographically, leave your details and a cancer support specialist will call you.

Finding out you have cancer can change your life — for you, your family, friends and work colleagues. Whatever kind of cancer and whatever stage you’re at, Maggie’s will be here for you, both in our cancer centres and online.

Alzheimer’s Society

1 in 3 people born in the UK today will develop dementia in their lifetime. It affects all of us, every member of society. Please donate to the Forget Me Not Appeal to help end the devastation caused by dementia. Contact the Alzheimer’s Society on 0330 333 0804 or visit alzheimers.org.uk.

Every Forget Me Not carries its own meaning. You might wear yours for someone you love, for someone you’ve lost, or for the hope of a future without dementia. Whatever your reason, every badge tells a story; every donation is a step closer to beating dementia.
