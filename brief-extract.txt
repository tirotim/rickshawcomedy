BIOGRAPHY
Rick Shaw (stage name) is a hugely talented Stand-Up Comedian, Mimic and Comedic Imitator who first burst onto the comedy scene in the 1980’s / 1990’s winning Welsh Impressionist of the Year, appearing with Russell Grant and Anne Aston (The Golden Shot / Bob Monkhouse) on the television programme ‘All Kinds of Everything’. 
Rick Shaw was also one of Fagin’s boys in Lional Bart’s Oliver Twist and in the 1980’s was the manic front man for Wales’ Top Comedy Showband. Their two-hour show was a mainframe choice in entertaining British Officers and their partners at Christmas time, celebrating their Annual Regiments’ Dinner/Dance in Germany.
Rick Shaw is still a prolific comedy writer and in the late 1990’s penned a plethora of comedy material and sketches. Unfortunately, a different career choice was pursued out of the entertainment industry and many of his ‘alter-ego’ characters like ‘Fame Edinbra Beverage’(Dame Edna Everage), ‘Wax Moyst’ (Sir Max Boyce), ‘Luigi (Loo) Brush’, ‘Heinze Beenze’, Hank Frencher (Frank Spencer), Commy Trooper (Tommy Cooper), Spruce Forsight (Sir Bruce Forsyth) and Cilly Bonnelly (Sir Billy Connelly) were retired a couple of years into the new Millennium.
But… hey, there’s life in the old dog yet! Over the last four years he’s retired early to hone his comedy writing and performing skills once more. In 2023 his hour-long show ‘Rick Shaw & Friends’ was offered a ‘twenty-four night come-back tour’ to appear for promoters ‘Just the Tonic’ at the Edinburgh Fringe. Unfortunately, he became very unwell during this time and although slowly recovering decided not to do the festival; mainly because of his health, but also the high costs associated in taking a show to Edinburgh. It’s still on his bucket list though!
Rick Shaw Comedy tends to cater for audiences who are over 40 years of age and hence appreciate some of the vintage comedy gems from yester-year. By his own admission some of his material is not ‘politically correct’ and as such he will not be everyone’s cup of tea! 
However, he is bringing some of Britain’s best loved comedy treasures back to life and immerses himself in the old school ‘retro’ comedy and musical legends who are and were entertainment greats.
Rick Shaw is a ‘feel-good entertainer’ performing his ‘Nostalgic Knight Set’ taking us all on a trip down memory lane, honouring comic and musical royalty from a bygone era. Furthermore, whilst now dormant, his alter-ego characters were originally concepted and written for television. 
Like many comedians form the 70’, 80’ and 90’s Rick Shaw has a huge comedy collection, much of which was written by himself over the past fifty years. During 2026/27 he will be celebrating 50 years as a performer and is presently writing and rehearsing a two-hour ‘Nostalgic Knights Show to tour next year.

‘Nostalgic Knights’ – Comedy Entertainers


FAME  EDIN-BRA  BEVERAGE   AS   DAME  EDNA  EVERAGE   (Australian flag and caricature). 

Rick Shaws’ alter ego ‘Fame Edin-Bra Beverage’ is of course the Barry Humphries Australian housewife and megastar ‘Dame Edna Everage’.
Known for her lilac-coloured wig, outlandish spectacles and her exuberant greeting ‘Hello Possums’ she became well and truly loved by Northern Hemisphere audiences especially in the UK. Dame Edna achieving cult celebrity comedy status appearing on television programmes, an audience with and appearing on the Royal Variety Performance in front of King Charles and Queen Camilla.
Rick Shaw is renowned as being one of the ‘world’s funniest’ Dame Edna Everage impersonators. JESTER HAT BUTTON FOR AUDIO.



SPRUCE  FORESIGHT   AS   SIR  BRUCE  FORSYTH    (English flag and caricature).

Rick Shaws’ alter ego Spruce Foresight is a humorous spoof about the English Entertainer and Presenter whose career lasted more than 75 years.
Best known for his catch phrase ‘Nice to see you – to see you nice’  Bruce Forsyth was the beloved host on many well-known TV programmes such as ‘The Generation Game’, ‘Play Your Cards Right’, ‘The Price Is Right’, ‘You Bet’, and of course ‘Sunday Night at the London Palladium’.
But probably Sir Bruce Forsyth is best known as one of the co-presenters of ‘Strictly Come Dancing’ from 2004 to 2013.
A keen golfer, he was one of Britain’s most respected entertainers, song and dance men and loveable legends. His two-hour show ‘Bruce Forsyth’s Big Night In’ was a huge hit with the British public because of its different entertainment formats.
JESTER HAT BUTTON FOR AUDIO.


COMMY  TROOPER   AS   SIRE  TOMMY  COOPER   (Welsh flag and caricature).

Rick Shaws’ alter ego Commy Trooper pays homage to probably one of Britains best-loved comedians and magician’s, Tommy Cooper. Whilst he was never knighted to become a ‘Sir’ many respected modern-day comedians and entertainers believe a posthumous award should be forthcoming to acknowledge a true great amongst greats. 
Oh rise – Sire Tommy Cooper!
Born in Caerphilly, South Wales, he habitually wore a red fez when performing and was a member of the Magic Circle.
Many of his catch phrases such as ‘Just Like That’ were developed entertaining the troops during his military service in the second world war. He became known for his two-liner silly gags and magic tricks that seemingly went wrong but, in fact showed off his comedic genius. 
Tommy Cooper rose to become one of the Uk’s highest paid entertainers of his time, headlining his own shows as well as appearing as a guest star on major entertainment programmes of that nostalgic era. JESTER HAT BUTTON FOR AUDIO.



SIR  MICHAEL  CRAWFORD   AS   PRANK  FRENCER  (Frank Spencer)
(English flag and caricature).

Originated by Sir Michael Crawford, the hapless Frank Spencer appeared in the hilarious sit-com ‘Some Mothers Do Have Them’.
With his raincoat and French black berrey hat his catch phrase ‘Oh Betty’ referred to his long-suffering wife (Michele Dotrice).
Rick Shaws alter ego ‘Prank Frencher’ reminds us how popular this slapstick comedian was during the 70’s and 80’s mainly doing his own stunts and hysterical physical comedy.
Sir Michael Crawford went on to be a huge Broadway Star and London Palladium Stalwart appearing in such shows as ‘Phantom of the Opera’, ‘Billy Liar’, ‘PT Barnum’ as well as  ‘Hello Dolly’ with Barbara Streisand.
JESTER HAT BUTTON FOR AUDIO.





HANKIE  FORWARD  AS   FRANKIE  HOWERD  OBE (English flag and caricature).

Hankie Forward alias Frankie Howerd OBE is one of Rick Shaws’ original creations dating back to the late 70’s and 80’s. 
As with many of his ‘Nostalgic Knights’ their career started in the military during the second world war. In fact Frankie Howerd OBE was part of the D-Day efforts on June 6th 1944.
He became a comedy music hall great with his masterful monolgues and brilliant catchphrases such as ‘Titter Ye Not’, ‘Oh Please Yourselves’ and Oh No Missus.
He became synonymous for his variety shows and the television series ‘Up Pompeii’ where he acted as the character Lurcio with his utterly brilliant double entendres. 
In the 70’s he appeared in the big budget Hollywood film ‘Srgt., Peppers Lonely Heart Club Band’; appearing alongside Peter Frampton, The Bee Gees, George Burns, Alice Cooper, Aerosmith and Steve Martin. He returned to our TV screens once again in the late 80’s with Cilla Black and his good friend Benny Hill.

CILLY  BONNOLLY  AS  SIR  BILLY  CONNELLY  (Scottish flag and caricature).

Rick Shaw has reincarnated his alter-ego character Cilly Bonnelly in his own idiosyncratic way with clever improvised observational humour and Scottish comedy.
Without any doubt whatsoever, Sir Billy Connelly is one of the best entertainers to ever come out of the UK. He is an acclaimed stand-up comedian and actor who has plied his trade all over the world especially in the UK and the USA where he now lives.  An accomplished singer, comic, writer and poet, he reached No 1. In the UK singles chart with his own comedic version of  D.I.V.O.R.C.E.
Sir Billy Connolly appeared in numerous blockbuster films including The Last Samarie, The Hobbit, The X Files, Muppet Treasure Island and Indecent Proposal. He was a regular guest on chat shows such as Parkinson, Wogan and Jonathan Ross. Regarded as entertainment royalty he counts Sir Elton John and other mega-celebrities amongst his friends.

TIMMY   WICKET   AS   JIMMY   CRICKET  (Papal Knighthood) (Saint Patricks Saltire flag and caricature).

Jimmy Cricket is a Northern Ireland comedian, who first came to prominence in the 1970’s and had his own shows on television and radio. One of Rick Shaw’s most loved comedians Jimmy Cricket was awarded a Papal Knighthood (Order of St. Gregory the Great) by Pope Francis for all his superb charity work.
Rick Shaw embraces the popular theme of Irish Logic with his alter-ego character Timmy Wicket and has re-written the ubiquitous letter from his ‘mammy’!  His catch phrase ‘Ladies and Gentlemen – come here – and there’s more’ became as synonymous as did his wellington boots labelled left and right!
He appeared in a number of Royal Variety Performances, The Queens Platinum Jubilee Show; as well as TV’s long running entertainment show ‘The Good Old Days’. He was also one of a number of performers who appeared in the ‘Comic Releief’ video for The Proclaimers song ‘I’m Gonna Be 500 Miles’ written by Matt Lucas, Andy Pipkin, and the legendary Peter Kay as Brian Potter.
